## Review against the jobs-to-be-done and the landing page — what to add (Round 37: 1–7 shipped; 8 waits for real stories; 9 dropped — numbers without a source are left out)

The owner's jobs-to-be-done (Round 32): **Save time · Feel good about it · Make money**. The Round 36 landing covers
"feel good" well (clarity, misfit, parents, past choices, mentors) and "make money" well (pay, demand, AI, master's,
switching cost). Gaps:

1. **Save time is thin.** Nothing says how long the whole thing takes. Add a question: *"How long does it take?"* —
   about 60–75 minutes of counselling, about 81 minutes of assessment, saved as you go, at your own pace; the
   5-minute demo first.
2. **Price worries aren't answered on the landing.** Financial aid and "don't like your results? we refund you, on
   any plan" exist but sit only on the programs page and the program finder. Add *"What if I can't afford it — or
   don't like my results?"* (no amounts in copy).
3. **Parents.** For Class 9 to 12 the parent often pays and decides, and the under-18 flow already emails them for
   consent. A short "For parents" set (or a parents' deck) — what we ask, what we never share, how the report helps
   the conversation at home — would serve the real buyer.
4. **Privacy and inclusion as reasons to trust.** Two built differences aren't on the landing: the résumé is read once
   and never stored, answers are never shared; and a student who tells us about a difficulty (hearing, reading,
   attention…) has the timed tests set aside — "not measured", never low, and it never ranks a career. One question
   each: *"Who sees my answers?"* and *"I have a learning difficulty — will it count against me?"*
5. **The program finder isn't linked from the landing.** "Let's figure out your career" goes to /programs; the new
   /find-your-program asks one question and shows only the plans that fit. Point the hero button (or a second link)
   there.
6. **"Coming soon" vs "by invitation only".** How it works calls the skill kit, internships/jobs and master's/abroad
   help "by invitation only"; the marketing calls them "coming soon". Pick one wording for both.
7. **"Personalized tracking"** in "Why we're different" (vs AI tools) doesn't name a feature a student can find.
   Replace it with something built — e.g. "your report updates when you do".
8. **Proof.** There are no student stories on the landing. Add them only when real ones exist (Success stories page).
9. **Three numbers need sources** before they can be shown: 90% rely on parents and friends; 50% regret their
   choices by 25; 70% say their job isn't what they expected (HANDOVER Part 3).
