## STORY 1 — AARAV, Class 11 → Product Designer

### 🧾 What Aarav told us
- **Interests:** sketching, tinkering with gadgets, video game level design, watching "how it's
  made" videos
- **Activities he'd actually done:** rebuilt his cycle's gears, modded his PC case, ran the
  school fest stage-design team for two years
- **Problems he cared about:** "things that look good but are annoying to use"
- **A belief he held:** "I learn by building, not by reading theory"

### 🧠 Psychometric strengths that shaped the match
| Strength | Signal |
|---|---|
| Spatial intelligence | High |
| Divergent thinking / creativity | High |
| Bodily / hands-on | High |
| Openness | High |
| Interest in abstract theory | Low |

### 🎯 What we recommended (ranked to *him*)
1. **Product / Industrial Designer** ✅ *(chose this)*
2. Robotics & Mechatronics Engineer
3. UX / Interaction Designer
4. Architect
5. Automotive Designer

> Everyone had told Aarav "just do software engineering." His profile said otherwise — generic
> software was a *weak* fit for how he's wired. His spatial-creative-hands-on combination made
> **design and build-oriented engineering** the natural home.

### 🚀 Where he is now
> Aarav is in a **product design** program, building real things, and — in his words —
> "finally studying something where the way my brain works is the whole point, not a problem."

## STORY 2 — MEERA, College 2nd year → Sustainability Analyst

### 🧾 What Meera told us
- **Interests:** nature documentaries, organising community clean-ups, spreadsheets (genuinely),
  reading about climate policy
- **Activities she'd actually done:** ran her college's waste-segregation drive, interned at a
  local NGO, maintained her family's monthly budget for years
- **Problems she cared about:** "we know what's wrong with the planet but not how to make the
  fixes actually pay"
- **A belief she held:** "impact and income don't have to be opposites"

### 🧠 Psychometric strengths that shaped the match
| Strength | Signal |
|---|---|
| Naturalistic intelligence | High |
| Logical + reasoning | High |
| Conscientiousness | High |
| Existential (meaning-driven) | High |
| Convergent thinking (structured problem-solving) | High |

### 🎯 What we recommended (ranked to *her*)
1. Environmental Scientist
2. **ESG / Sustainability Analyst** ✅ *(chose this)*
3. Urban & Environmental Planner
4. Green-Finance Analyst
5. Conservation Program Manager

> Meera assumed "caring about the environment" meant a low-paying NGO track. Her strong
> logical-analytical-conscientious profile pointed at the **analytical, in-demand, financially
> solid** end of sustainability — a corner she didn't know existed.

### 🚀 Where she is now
> Meera is training as a **sustainability analyst**, combining the cause she cares about with
> the numbers she's good at — "the part I thought I had to give up to do good."

*(Illustrative example.)*
